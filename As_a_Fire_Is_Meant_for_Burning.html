Reading your sheet

ring
Boxed: strike your bell on this word.
stop
Underlined: damp your bell on this word, because your note is not in the new chord.
rest
No mark: do nothing. Your note isn’t due to ring again yet.

Before you start

  • You need bells C, D, E, F, G, A, Bb, one per ringer.
  • Begin with a 2-beat pickup. In 6/4 time, beats 1, 3, 5 force a full-chord strike. Play the full chord on the opening note, including the lead-in. The first note after each breath also forces a full-chord strike. At every chord change, strike the full new chord and damp bells from the previous chord that are not included, even within a measure. The melody bell strikes on every note, including repeated pitches. On other beats, other bells strike only if the same bell is not also due to strike on the very next beat.
  • Unlabelled held-note positions have no lyric or ring marker.
  • Each lyric block uses the musical pattern with the same name as its type.
  • Short on ringers? One person can ring two bells, one in each hand.
  • To print, use your browser’s print command. Each bell gets its own page.

As a Fire Is Meant for Burning (F major)

Stanza 1

As a fire is meant for burn- ing, with a bright and warm- ing flame,

so the church is meant for mis- sion, giv- ing glo- ry to God's name.

Not to preach our creeds or cus- toms, but to build a bridge of care, ‖

we join hands a- cross the na- tions, find- ing neigh- bors ev- ery- wehre.

Stanza 2

We are learn- ers; we are teach- ers; we are pil- grims on the way.

We are seek- ers; we are giv- ers; we are ves- sels made of clay.

By our gen- tle, lov- ing ac- tions we would show that Christ is light. ‖

In a hum- ble, lis- tening Spir- it we would live to God's de- light.

Stanza 3

As a green bud in the spring- time is a sign of life re- newed,

so may we be signs of one- ness 'mid earth's peop- ples, man- y hued.

As a rain- bow lights the heav- ens when a storm is past and gone, ‖

may our lives re- flect the ra- diance of God's new and glo- rious dawn.
