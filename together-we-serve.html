Together We Serve

Handbell ring-along cue sheets for 7 bells, 3/4 time. Built locally from your song file.

Reading your sheet

ring
Boxed: strike your bell on this word.
stop
Underlined: damp your bell on this word, because your note is not in the new chord.
rest
No mark: do nothing. Your note isn’t due to ring again yet.

Before you start

  • You need bells Eb4, F4, G4, A4, Bb4, C5, D5, one per ringer.
  • Begin with a 1-beat pickup. In 3/4 time, beats 1, 3 force a full-chord strike. Play the full chord on the opening note, including the lead-in. The first note after each breath also forces a full-chord strike. At every chord change, strike the full new chord and damp bells from the previous chord that are not included, even within a measure. The melody bell strikes on every note, including repeated pitches. On other beats, other bells strike only if the same bell is not also due to strike on the very next beat.
  • Unlabelled held-note positions have no lyric or ring marker.
  • Marks repeat in every stanza, because every stanza uses the same music.
  • Short on ringers? One person can ring two bells, one in each hand.
  • To print, use your browser’s print command. Each bell gets its own page.
