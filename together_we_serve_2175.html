Together We Serve 2175 (Bb major)

Handbell ring-along cue sheets for 7 bells, 3/4 time. Built locally from your song file.

Reading your sheet

ring
Boxed: strike your bell on this word.
stop
Underlined: damp your bell on this word, because your note is not in the new chord.
rest
No mark: do nothing. Your note isn’t due to ring again yet.

Before you start

  • You need bells Eb, F, G, A, Bb, C, D, one per ringer.
  • Begin with a 1-beat pickup. In 3/4 time, beats 1, 3 force a full-chord strike. Play the full chord on the opening note, including the lead-in. The first note after each breath also forces a full-chord strike. At every chord change, strike the full new chord and damp bells from the previous chord that are not included, even within a measure. The melody bell strikes on every played note, including repeated pitches, unless explicitly excluded. Per-note exclusions remove bells from the chord and damp them if already sounding. On other beats, other bells strike only if the same bell is not also due to strike on the very next beat.
  • Unlabelled held-note positions have no lyric or ring marker.
  • Each lyric block uses the musical pattern with the same name as its type.
  • Short on ringers? One person can ring two bells, one in each hand.
  • To print, use your browser’s print command. Each bell gets its own page.
