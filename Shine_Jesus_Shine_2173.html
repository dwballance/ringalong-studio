Before you start

  • You need bells C#, D, E, F#, G, G#, A, B, one per ringer.
  • Begin on beat 1 of a full measure. In 4/4 time, beats 1, 3 force a full-chord strike. Play the full chord on the opening note, including the lead-in. The first note after each breath also forces a full-chord strike. At every chord change, strike the full new chord and damp bells from the previous chord that are not included, even within a measure. The melody bell strikes on every note, including repeated pitches. On other beats, other bells strike only if the same bell is not also due to strike on the very next beat.
  • Unlabelled held-note positions have no lyric or ring marker.
  • Each lyric block uses the musical pattern with the same name as its type.
  • Short on ringers? One person can ring two bells, one in each hand.
  • To print, use your browser’s print command. Each bell gets its own page.
